Hello! I am Chengbo (Nicholas) Zhang, a PhD student in Transportation Engineering at McGill University, supervised by Prof. Jiangbo Yu. I work on human–AI interaction for urban mobility and city science: how people and AI agents plan, move and decide together in cities.

I build large-language-model agents whose personas come from real trajectories, reconstruct city-scale movement of on-demand delivery riders, and measure how street environments shape walking, jogging and cycling. My papers appear in npj Sustainable Mobility and Transport, Cities, Transportation and the Journal of Transport Geography.

I am the founder and director of Agentic City Lab, which studies how people and AI agents plan, move and decide together in cities. Before McGill I studied urban planning at Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen) and Xiamen University.
