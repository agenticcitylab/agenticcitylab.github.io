Agentic City Lab · people and AI agents, moving together in cities

Human–AI interaction for urban mobility and city science

People AI agents Request and reply Interaction

Interaction logsimulated

    Cities now run on human–AI interaction. Couriers work with dispatch algorithms, cyclists follow navigation apps, and language-model agents can plan a walk, a trip or a policy. Every day, people and AI agents make decisions for, with and about each other.

    Agentic City Lab studies these interactions. We ground agent models in real mobility data, measure how algorithms change the way people move, and design human–AI mobility systems that stay efficient, fair and legible to the people who live with them.

    We work on three connected fronts: how people move through urban environments, how their movements form networks and logistics systems, and how agentic AI can reason and act alongside them. The lab is directed by Chengbo (Nicholas) Zhang.
